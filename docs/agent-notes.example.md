# My rules for agents (an example: every name and address here is made up)

Point AGENT_NOTES_FILE at a file like this one. The server adds it to its instructions under "OWNER'S OWN RULES". Keep it on
your own machine: it is never part of the package.

## Calendars
- Doctor, dentist and physio go on "Health"; job interviews and anything for work on "Work"; everything else on "Personal".
- Invite my partner, Robin (robin@example.org), to anything that needs both of us; never write their name in the title.
- I cycle everywhere in town: use 15 minutes of travel time for places in the city centre, unless I say otherwise.

## Mail
- Sign mail to people I know with "Sam", and formal mail with "Sam Example".
- Messages to friends only between 08:00 and 22:00; to organisations only on weekdays.
- Newsletters from shop.example.com can be archived without asking.

## Reminders and notes

  personal goes there.
